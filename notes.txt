Reserved Instance

Example: Switching After 4 Months (m5.large → c5.large)
Initial Plan (m5.large)
Total cost: $550/year
Paid for 4 months: $183
Remaining value for 8 months: $367

New Plan (c5.large)
Total cost: $600/year
Cost for remaining 8 months: $400

Cost Adjustment Calculation
Extra cost = $400 - $367 = $33

Instead of a one-time payment, AWS spreads this over the remaining 8 months:
33÷8=4.13 USD extra per month

New Monthly Cost After Switching
Old cost: $45.83/month
New cost: $50/month ($45.83 + $4.13)

Scenarios If You Don't Use the Instance
1️⃣ Standard RI (Fixed Commitment)
You still pay the full amount even if you don’t use the instance.
AWS does not refund or pause your RI.
Example:
You bought m5.large Standard RI for 1 year at $550.
You don’t use it at all.
You still pay $550 for the entire year.

Convertible RI (Flexible, But Still Charged)
You still pay for it, even if you don’t use it.
But, you can convert it to another instance family/type.
Example:
You bought m5.large Convertible RI for 1 year at $550.
You don’t use it but need c5.large instead.
You convert it to c5.large and continue using the new instance.


Can You Cancel or Sell an Unused RI?
✔ For Standard RIs

You CANNOT cancel it.
But, you can sell it on the AWS Reserved Instance Marketplace if it’s a Standard RI (1-year or 3-year term paid fully upfront).
AWS allows you to list your RI for sale, and if someone buys it, you get your money back.
✔ For Convertible RIs

You CANNOT sell them.
But, you can convert to a different instance type that you might need.
